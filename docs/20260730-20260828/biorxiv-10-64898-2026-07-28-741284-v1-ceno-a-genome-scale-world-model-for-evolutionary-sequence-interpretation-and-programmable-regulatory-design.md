## 摘要
DNA编码的生物功能跨越一系列序列尺度，从单核苷酸和基序级语法到调控邻域、染色质规模组织以及进化约束。因此，一个有用的基因组模型不应仅仅局限于对短序列窗口进行分类：它应在长上下文中保持核苷酸分辨率的状态，对反事实突变进行评分，以同源序列证据为条件，并生成可根据结构或功能目标进行评估的候选序列。我们在操作上将这样的系统定义为基因组世界模型：一种基因组序列空间的通用生成模型，通过共享状态和似然接口统一序列理解与序列设计。在此，我们介绍CENO，一个长上下文生成式基因组世界模型家族，旨在保留局部DNA语法的同时，将可用上下文扩展到调控和染色质尺度。CENO在单个自回归骨干网络中结合了Mamba序列混合层、稀疏注意力层和混合专家容量，并以300M、600M和1B参数规模进行训练，采用从8k-token跨域基因组预训练到131k和1M token全基因组长上下文延续的分阶段课程。我们在一个统一的世界模型基准范式下评估CENO，涵盖检索、表示、反事实扰动、重构、进化条件化和设计。CENO保持了实用的长上下文推理，并在合成实验中检索远端序列。在零样本长上下文分析中，无需任务特定微调，长上下文延续产生了与注释和染色质边界相关的注意力模式，以及跨人类细胞类型和小鼠细胞或组织设置泛化的冻结状态表示。为了整合进化信息，我们进一步在打包的真实多序列比对上下文上对CENO进行后训练，并通过参考-突变似然差对变异进行评分，从而改进匹配的变异效应预测，并在物种间产生进化富集信号。作为这些基于扰动的变异测试的补充，我们通过部分基因延续评估零样本长序列生成，询问模型能否跨真核、细菌和古菌物种恢复被遮蔽的基因尺度序列结构；恢复效果随模型规模及后续全基因组长上下文训练而提升。最后，我们将CENO作为小鼠皮层细胞类型特异性增强子设计工作流程的骨干，将基于CENO的可及性预言机与条件监督微调及预言机引导的强化学习相结合。总之，CENO为序列解释、进化推理、基因尺度重构和可编程调控序列生成提供了一个基因组尺度的序列世界模型框架。

## Abstract
DNA encodes biological function across a continuum of sequence scales, from single-nucleotide and motif-level grammar to regulatory neighborhoods, chromatin-scale organization and evolutionary constraint. A useful model of genomes should therefore do more than classify short sequence windows: it should maintain nucleotide-resolution state over long contexts, score counterfactual mutations, condition on homologous sequence evidence and generate candidates that can be evaluated against structural or functional objectives. We define such a system operationally as a genomic world model: a general-purpose generative model of genome sequence space that unifies sequence understanding and sequence design through a shared state and likelihood interface. Here we introduce CENO, a family of long-context generative genomic world models designed to preserve local DNA grammar while extending usable context to regulatory and chromatin scales. CENO combines Mamba sequence-mixing layers, sparse attention layers and mixture-of-experts capacity in a single autoregressive backbone, and is trained at 300M, 600M and 1B parameter scales with a staged curriculum that progresses from 8k-token cross-domain genomic pretraining to 131k- and 1M-token whole-genome long-context continuation. We evaluate CENO under a unified world-model benchmark paradigm spanning retrieval, representation, counterfactual perturbation, reconstruction, evolutionary conditioning and design. CENO retains practical long-context inference and retrieves distal sequence in synthetic assays. In zero-shot long-context analyses, without task-specific fine-tuning, long-context continuation yields annotation- and chromatin-boundary-associated attention patterns and frozen-state representations that generalize across human cell types and mouse cell or tissue settings. To incorporate evolutionary information, we further post-train CENO on packed real multiple-sequence-alignment contexts and score variants by reference-mutant likelihood deltas, improving matched variant-effect prediction and producing evolutionary enrichment signals across species. Complementing these perturbation-based variant tests, we evaluate zero-shot long-sequence generation by partial-gene continuation, asking whether the model can recover withheld gene-scale sequence structure across eukaryotic, bacterial and archaeal species; recovery improves with model scale and later whole-genome long-context training. Finally, we use CENO as the backbone for a cell-type-specific enhancer design workflow in mouse cortex, coupling a CENO-based accessibility oracle with conditional supervised fine-tuning and oracle-guided reinforcement learning. Together, CENO provides a genome-scale sequence world-model framework for sequence interpretation, evolutionary reasoning, gene-scale reconstruction and programmable regulatory sequence generation.
