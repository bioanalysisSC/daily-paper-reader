## 摘要
在变化环境中进化既需要可靠表达当前有利表型，这以外显率为量化指标，也需要通过突变达到替代表型的能力。先前研究表明高外显率可能限制这种突变途径。然而，由于这些研究集中于已进化的基因型和局部突变邻域，它们无法确定这种局部约束是否限制了环境变化下的突变适应性。这种适应性取决于基因型相对于其他环境高适应度区域的位置。解决这一问题需要重建每种基因型上表型的完整概率分布，以及由此产生的跨基因型空间的环境特异性适应度景观。这种重建通常不可行，因为基因型和表型空间呈组合式增长。本文中，一个受统计物理中相互作用自旋启发的随机基因型-表型映射抽象模型，允许对该映射进行穷尽式重建。我们发现高外显率基因型倾向于占据环境特异性高适应度区域的内部，且具有突变稳健性，而低外显率基因型倾向于位于其边界附近，对替代环境的高适应度区域具有更大的突变可达性。这种整体几何学产生了外显率与突变适应性之间的权衡。在进化模拟中，固定环境中更强的表型噪声增加了可靠表达的选择优势，从而有利于高外显率和突变稳健性。频繁的环境变化则有利于突变可达性，但以外显率为代价。因此，外显率和适应性是同一整体几何学的对立结果，环境条件决定了它们的进化平衡。

## Abstract
Evolution in changing environments requires both reliable expression of the currently favored phenotype, as quantified by penetrance, and the capacity to reach alternative phenotypes through mutation. Previous studies suggest that high penetrance may restrict such mutational access. However, because these studies focus on evolved genotypes and local mutational neighborhoods, they cannot determine whether this local constraint limits mutational adaptability under environmental change. Such adaptability depends on a genotype's position relative to high-fitness regions for other environments. Addressing this question requires reconstructing the full probability distribution over phenotypes for every genotype and the resulting environment-specific fitness landscapes across genotype space. Such reconstruction is generally infeasible because genotype and phenotype spaces grow combinatorially. Here, an abstract model of stochastic genotype-phenotype mapping, inspired by interacting spins in statistical physics, permits exhaustive reconstruction of the map. We find that high-penetrance genotypes tend to occupy the interior of environment-specific high-fitness regions and are mutationally robust, whereas lower-penetrance genotypes tend to lie near their boundaries and have greater mutational access to high-fitness regions for alternative environments. This global geometry generates a trade-off between penetrance and mutational adaptability. In evolutionary simulations, stronger phenotypic noise in a fixed environment increases the selective advantage of reliable expression, thereby favoring high penetrance and mutational robustness. Frequent environmental change instead favors mutational accessibility at the expense of penetrance. Thus, penetrance and adaptability are opposing consequences of the same global geometry, with environmental conditions determining their evolutionary balance.
